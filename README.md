# NBA Watch Tracker

![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/filippospol/nba-watch-tracker)
![Last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/filippospol/nba-watch-tracker)
![License](https://img.shields.io/github/license/filippospol/nba-watch-tracker)

NBA Watch Tracker is where you log the games you've watched and keep a running record of your season. Scores stay hidden until you reveal them, tip-offs show in your own time zone, and your stats build up as you go. It's a record of your season as a fan — the games, the teams, the nights you'll want to remember.
